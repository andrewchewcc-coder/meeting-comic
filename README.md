# Meeting Cartoon

Paste meeting notes or a transcript and get a one-page comic strip (4–6 panels) that sums up what was discussed, what was decided, and who does what next.

## How to open it

1. Download `index.html` (on GitHub: open the file, then click the download button).
2. Double-click it. It opens in your web browser. Nothing else to install.
3. Click **Show me an example** to see a sample comic straight away.

## Making your own comic

1. Open **Settings: your Claude API key** and paste a key from https://console.anthropic.com (one-time setup).
2. Paste your notes into the big box. Notes like `Priya: ...` work, and so do bullet points.
3. Click **Make my comic**. It takes about 20–60 seconds.
4. Check the **Official Recap** box at the bottom against your meeting, then click **Download comic as image**.

Each comic costs a few cents of API usage. Your key is saved only in this browser on this computer and is sent only to Anthropic.
